Purpose
This document is the working brief for the web developer. The website should establish one strong NAWEZA CONSULTING brand while clearly distinguishing the two underlying legal entities. The website is intended to evolve over time; the immediate priority is to establish the domain, professional domain email, core positioning and a credible structure that can later accommodate online bookings, payments, client onboarding and additional services.

8. Photography & Visual Direction
The visual identity should be clean, modern and corporate rather than looking like a generic sales flyer. Preferred palette: corporate blue, black, white and off-white. Avoid heavy gold/green styling and excessive icons.
Primary founder / team photography: professional upper-body corporate portraits; natural, confident and approachable.
Working images: laptop/research, reviewing documents, meetings, planning sessions and business discussions.
Africa market imagery: Namibia/Africa maps, logistics, ports, industrial sites, agriculture, renewable energy, construction/mining equipment and commercial activity.
International imagery: subtle Africa-to-Asia / Africa-to-global visual cues where relevant.
Case-study imagery: use client/project photographs only where permission exists.
Avoid generic handshake photographs, overly staged stock photos and visuals that imply services we do not provide.
9. Legal / Entity Presentation
The website should make the distinction clear without making the homepage feel legally complicated.
Naweza Investments CC — operating/established since 2018.
Naweza Consulting Investments (Pty) Ltd — established 2026.
The Pty Ltd should not be presented as registered before its actual registration is completed.
Once registration is complete, add the Pty Ltd's official registration details to the relevant footer/legal/contact area.
Where a specific service is contractually provided by one entity, the quotation, invoice, agreement and other formal documents should identify the correct legal entity.
10. International Client Acquisition — Immediate Execution
Do NOT wait for the website to be perfect before beginning international outreach. The domain and professional email are the immediate priority. LinkedIn outreach can begin using the Pty Ltd's 2026 identity while the website is being completed.
If a client responds positively or a paid opportunity arises before Pty Ltd registration is completed, do not misrepresent the company as already registered. Explain transparently that the new entity's registration is being finalised and confirm the appropriate contracting/invoicing entity before accepting the engagement.
For a high-value or hourly international engagement, the legal entity that will sign the agreement and receive payment should be confirmed before work formally starts. Once the Pty Ltd registration is complete, provide the official registration documents when reasonably requested by the client.

13. Developer Notes
Build the site so the content can be edited easily as services and company information evolve.
Mobile-first design is essential.
Use a professional CMS so pages, services, case studies and booking information can be updated without rebuilding the site.
Keep the homepage concise; deeper information belongs on dedicated pages.
Use clear calls to action: Book a Consultation / Discuss a Project / Request Research.
Do not overload the website with icons, animations or stock imagery.
The website should feel credible to both a Namibian SME and an international corporate prospect.
